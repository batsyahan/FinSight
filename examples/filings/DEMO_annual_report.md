# DEMO Sample Corp: Annual Report FY2026 (SYNTHETIC, for offline demos)

--- page 1 ---
Letter to shareholders. FY2026 was a year of disciplined execution. Revenue grew 23.4 percent,
driven by the industrial automation segment, while we continued to invest in our AI-driven
operations platform. Our board approved a dividend and a share buyback programme.

--- page 2 ---
Management discussion and analysis. The industrial automation segment contributed 61 percent of
revenue. The services segment declined 4 percent as two large contracts ended. We expect revenue
growth of 15 to 18 percent in FY2027, with operating margin improving by 150 basis points as
automation investments mature. Guidance assumes stable input costs.

--- page 3 ---
Key risks. Free cash flow was negative this year because of elevated capital expenditure on new
plants; we expect it to turn positive in FY2027. Other risks include customer concentration (our
top five customers are 38 percent of revenue), currency movements, and a regulatory notice received
in October regarding disclosure practices, to which we have responded.

--- page 4 ---
Capital allocation. Capital expenditure peaked at 9 percent of revenue in FY2026 and is planned to
fall to 5 percent in FY2027. Net debt remains low. We intend to fund growth from internal accruals.
